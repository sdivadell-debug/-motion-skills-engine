# Freelance Client Growth & Revenue Allocation

This integration documents an owner-controlled workflow for finding suitable freelance clients, preparing proposals, coordinating work, and tracking a fixed revenue allocation.

## Approved allocation per $50 of eligible collected revenue

- **$5 (10%)** — reserve toward the user's Claude Max subscription.
- **$10 (20%)** — tools, websites, apps, hosting, and other business-capability upgrades.
- **$35 (70%)** — owner's remainder.

For amounts that are not multiples of $50, allocate proportionally. The calculation is based on cleared receipts after refunds/chargebacks and direct platform/transaction fees, not unpaid invoices or promised payments. Taxes and legal withholdings must be tracked separately.

## Important distinctions

- The Claude Max bucket is a savings reserve; $5 does not itself buy a subscription. Check current official pricing and obtain approval before changing a plan.
- Tool investment means purchases that improve the freelance business, not financial investments. No stocks, crypto, betting, interest, or speculative trading.
- Unless connected to a real financial account, balances are bookkeeping reserves only; the skill cannot hold or move money.
- The owner approves client outreach, bids, contracts, purchases, subscriptions, deployment, publication, and final delivery.
