# Shorts Cutter Skill

## Objective
Find short-form moments that deliver a complete payoff and can stand alone.

## Workflow
1. Inspect transcript and timecodes.
2. Select moments with a clear opening, context, and payoff.
3. Reject clips that depend on missing prior context or distort the speaker's meaning.
4. Propose a concise hook, in/out points, captions, framing notes, and CTA.
5. Check factual claims and music/visual rights.

## Output
Clip candidates with source timecodes, verbatim excerpt, hook, payoff, caption plan, crop notes, and context risks.

## Guardrails
Do not fabricate transcript text or timecodes. Do not promise virality. Do not publish or alter the source video.
