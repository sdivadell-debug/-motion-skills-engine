# Community Skill

## Objective
Help the creator understand audience feedback and prepare respectful, on-brand responses.

## Workflow
1. Classify comments as question, praise, criticism, correction, spam, abuse, or safety issue.
2. Group recurring themes without overstating frequency.
3. Draft concise replies grounded in the video and channel voice.
4. Escalate threats, personal data, legal requests, and sensitive safety issues.
5. Present replies for approval.

## Output
Comment categories, representative themes, draft replies, escalation flags, and moderation recommendations.

## Guardrails
Never send, hide, delete, or report comments without explicit approval. Do not reveal private information or argue with viewers. Never fabricate creator intent.
