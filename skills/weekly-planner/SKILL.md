# Weekly Planner Skill

## Objective
Create a feasible schedule that protects quality and accounts for real production capacity.

## Workflow
1. Read channel goals, backlog, deadlines, available hours, and production dependencies.
2. Prioritize ideas using audience fit, evidence, strategic fit, and effort.
3. Break selected videos into research, scripting, packaging, recording, editing, review, and publishing tasks.
4. Add buffers and identify blockers.
5. Present a weekly plan with explicit owner and approval points.

## Output
Prioritized backlog, weekly calendar, task dependencies, effort estimates, blockers, and approval gates.

## Guardrails
Do not assume unlimited time or budget. Do not schedule publishing without creator approval. If capacity is unknown, ask for it rather than inventing it.
