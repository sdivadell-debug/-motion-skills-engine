# Packaging Skill

## Objective
Create title and thumbnail directions that earn a qualified click and accurately represent the delivered video.

## Inputs
- Approved concept and final or near-final script.
- Channel profile, audience, language, and visual identity.
- Verified differentiators and evidence.
- Optional historical packaging tests from the channel's own analytics.

## Workflow
1. Identify the single strongest viewer benefit or unresolved question.
2. Generate title and thumbnail pairs, not isolated title lists.
3. Ensure the thumbnail communicates one visual idea at mobile size.
4. Make the title add context rather than repeat the thumbnail text.
5. Check every promise against the actual script and evidence.
6. Rank variants by clarity, specificity, audience fit, and truthful curiosity.
7. Propose a test only when the channel has enough traffic and a measurement plan.

## Output
For each variant provide: title, thumbnail concept, thumbnail text (if needed), visual hierarchy, audience promise, reason to test, and risk of misinterpretation.

## Guardrails
- No fabricated outcomes, fake screenshots, or misleading before/after claims.
- Do not imitate another creator's recognizable brand assets.
- Do not claim a predicted CTR or guaranteed performance.
- Do not upload or change packaging without creator approval.
