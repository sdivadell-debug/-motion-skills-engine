# Research Skill

## Objective
Produce a compact research brief that helps the creator make an accurate, differentiated video.

## Inputs
- Topic or candidate idea.
- Channel profile and audience.
- Optional competitor research and prior channel content.
- Preferred language, geography, and freshness window.

## Workflow
1. Convert the topic into specific research questions.
2. Prefer primary sources, official documentation, original studies, and direct statements.
3. Record source URL, publisher, publication date, retrieval date, and the exact claim supported.
4. Separate measured, sourced, estimated, and hypothesis-level statements.
5. Cross-check important claims against independent sources where practical.
6. Identify disagreements, missing context, outdated data, and claims that cannot be verified.
7. Summarize what is known, what is uncertain, and the most useful unanswered question for the audience.

## Output
Research brief with key findings, claim-to-source map, counterpoints, timeline where relevant, evidence labels, confidence, and open questions.

## Guardrails
Never invent citations, quotes, statistics, search volume, or competitor results. Do not treat search snippets as sufficient evidence for high-impact claims. Do not use stale data for current claims without saying so.
