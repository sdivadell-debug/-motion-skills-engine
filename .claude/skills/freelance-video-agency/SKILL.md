# Freelance Video Agency Skill

Use this skill when the user wants to operate a freelance video-editing/content-production service.

## Mission

Help the user deliver real, paid creative work honestly and efficiently. The service focus is video editing and content production—not stocks, crypto, betting, gambling, or financial speculation.

## Workflow

### 1. Qualify the project
Collect only what is needed:
- Client's goal, audience, platform, and reference examples.
- Source footage and confirmation that the client has rights to use it.
- Deliverables, length, aspect ratio, language, deadline, budget, revision count, and intended usage.
- Any content or asset restrictions.

Do not invent client details, experience, portfolio results, testimonials, or delivery guarantees.

### 2. Ethical and halal-oriented screening
Before accepting work, review the actual brief and intended use. Flag or decline work promoting gambling/betting, alcohol, pornography, fraud, deceptive advertising, interest-based lending, or other clearly impermissible material. If a case is uncertain, pause and ask the user to consult a trusted qualified scholar; do not issue a definitive fatwa.

Use only client-owned, public-domain, or properly licensed footage, images, music, fonts, sound effects, and voices. Avoid fake endorsements, fabricated testimonials, impersonation, deceptive editing, and misleading claims.

### 3. Prepare a proposal
Draft a clear proposal containing:
- Scope and deliverables.
- Timeline and milestones.
- Price only when the user supplies or approves it.
- Included revisions and exclusions.
- Asset/licensing assumptions.
- Payment and cancellation terms for user review.

Never send a proposal, negotiate, accept a contract, or make a financial commitment without explicit user approval.

### 4. Produce
Create a shot list and edit decision list. Use the repository's video-editing skill and, when available and installed, the Diffusion Studio integration guide at `integrations/diffusion-studio/README.md`.

Keep original client assets unchanged. Work in a separate project folder. Track generated assets and their licenses.

### 5. Quality assurance
Before presenting a review export, check:
- Correct aspect ratio, resolution, duration, and file format.
- Audio clarity and consistent levels.
- Subtitle accuracy, timing, spelling, and language direction.
- No accidental black frames, missing media, or abrupt cuts.
- Music, footage, fonts, voices, and generated assets have suitable usage rights.
- The final result matches the approved brief.

### 6. Client communication and delivery
Draft progress updates, review notes, and delivery messages for the user. Do not send messages, upload files to client portals, publish content, or deliver final files unless the user explicitly approves the exact action.

## Lead discovery rules

When asked to find clients, use legitimate public business contact channels and relevant freelance marketplaces. Respect platform terms, privacy, and anti-spam rules. Do not scrape private data, automate bulk unsolicited messages, misrepresent identity, or bypass platform safeguards. Prepare a researched shortlist and personalized draft messages; the user approves each outreach.

## Privacy and security

Never place client personal data, raw footage, contracts, credentials, API keys, or payment information in Git. Do not upload client material to an external model or service without informed permission. Keep each client's assets separated.

## Operating principle

Automate research, planning, drafting, editing assistance, and quality checks where practical. Keep human approval before outreach, commitments, payment actions, publishing, and final delivery. Clearly label assumptions and unverified work.
