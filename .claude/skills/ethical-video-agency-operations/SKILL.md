# Ethical Video Agency Operations

Use this skill when planning or operating the user's AI-assisted freelance video-production agency in Team-X.

## Operating model
Team-X is the proposed desktop organization manager. Do not claim it is installed or running unless verified. Read integrations/team-x/README.md before setup or operational instructions.

## Start small
Begin with:
- Owner / CEO: the user; final authority.
- COO: internal project and task coordination.
- Sales Researcher: public opportunity research, no outreach.
- Proposal Writer: drafts only.
- Creative Director: briefs and shot lists.
- Video Editor: editing assistance on approved assets.
- QA Reviewer: checks work before human review.

Add more roles only when a real workload justifies the extra model usage and complexity.

## Standard project lifecycle
1. Qualify the client and brief.
2. Screen the requested content and intended use for ethical/halal concerns.
3. Confirm rights to source footage and all supplied assets.
4. Define scope, deliverables, aspect ratio, language, deadline, price, and revision count.
5. Draft a proposal for user approval.
6. Create a project and assign internal tickets with clear acceptance criteria.
7. Produce drafts using approved assets and permitted tools.
8. Run QA for audio, subtitles, spelling, timing, framing, export integrity, and licensing.
9. Present a review copy to the user.
10. Deliver only after the user's explicit approval.

## Ethical and halal-oriented screening
Flag or decline work involving gambling/betting, alcohol promotion, pornography, fraud, deceptive claims, interest-based lending, stolen media, or unlicensed assets. Do not issue religious rulings. If permissibility is uncertain, pause and recommend asking a trusted qualified scholar.

Do not fabricate testimonials, portfolio items, client results, qualifications, or revenue forecasts.

## Privacy and security
Do not put client personal data, raw footage, contracts, credentials, API keys, or payment details in Git. Do not send confidential materials to cloud AI providers without informed permission. Prefer local inference where practical and verify Team-X privacy-tier settings.

## Authority boundaries
Agents may perform internal research, drafting, planning, and work on authorized assets. They must not send messages, submit proposals, negotiate, sign contracts, spend money, charge clients, publish, access client accounts, or deliver final files without explicit user approval.

## Cost controls
Use a small number of agents and short tasks first. Monitor usage and provider costs. Do not activate paid APIs, install additional services, or connect credentials without explicit user approval.
